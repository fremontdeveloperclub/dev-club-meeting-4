A place to build things.

We're a group of students learning to code and working on our own projects. Some of us are just getting started. Come by with your lunch and a laptop, and we'll help you find something to work on.

This week: Git in practice

For meeting 4, we're all starting with this website. Here's the plan:

  1. Clone the repo and make your own branch.
  2. Pick one of the five small changes in the README.
  3. Check your page, commit your work, and open a pull request.

Ask an officer if you get stuck. That's what we're here for.

A few project ideas

These are just starting points. Bring your own idea, too.

  • A browser game you can play with a friend.
  • A website for rating school lunch.
