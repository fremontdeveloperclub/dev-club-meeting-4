A little about fish.

Fish come in all sorts of shapes, sizes, and colors. Some live on coral reefs, while others spend their lives in a quiet pond. Here are a few worth getting to know.

How do fish breathe?

Most fish use gills to take oxygen from the water. As water passes over the gills, oxygen moves into the fish's blood.

A few familiar fish

  • Clownfish are best known for their orange color and white stripes.
  • Rainbow trout have a pink stripe running along their sides.
  • Goldfish are freshwater fish often kept in ponds and aquariums.
